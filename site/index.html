        How the replay works

        The readingsFour 30-day windows from turbine 35: the prediction window of a labelled fault event, the same month a year earlier, a normal month with a close call, and a window ending in a labelled normal event. Dates are the benchmark's anonymised ones.
        The flagA reading is flagged when sensor_210 sits outside its usual range for that wind speed and time of year, measured on the turbine's own normal training year. The range is set so that 1% of healthy training readings fall outside it, the same 99% rule as the project's thresholds. This stands in for the autoencoder, whose trained weights are not published here.
        The counter and the thresholdNormal-status readings add 1 when flagged and subtract 1 otherwise, never going below zero. Service, downtime and other statuses leave it unchanged and are drawn in grey. The default threshold is 72 readings, about 12 hours at 10-minute resolution, as in the benchmark. It counts readings, not days, so it does not need changing for a month-long window. The slider shows what a stricter or looser threshold would do.
